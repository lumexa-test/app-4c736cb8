How it works

Three steps, then it runs itself.

  • 01

    Connect the workspace

    Sign in with Slack OAuth. We request read access to channel names and permission to post files — nothing else. Revoke it from Slack at any time.

  • 02

    Write a prompt or drop an image

    Type the shot you want, or upload one still and let the motion pass do the rest. Pick aspect ratio and length before it queues.

  • 03

    Choose the channel

    Set a destination per project. When the render lands, the file and its prompt are posted there — you don’t have to watch a progress bar.

Text to video

One line in. A finished clip out.

Write the scene the way you’d brief a camera operator — subject, movement, light. VidSlack returns a graded MP4 with audio-safe silence, ready to cut or post as it is.

  • 5, 8 or 12 seconds at 9:16, 1:1 or 16:9.
  • Prompt history stays attached to every file, so a good shot is repeatable.
  • Queue position visible while it renders — typically 40–90 seconds at 1080p.

Image to video

Give it one still. It gives back motion.

Drop a product shot, a frame from a shoot, a sketch. VidSlack keeps the composition and adds the movement you describe — drift, parallax, condensation, wind.

  • PNG, JPG or WebP up to 20 MB, any aspect.
  • Composition locked so packaging and type stay readable.
  • Uploads deleted after 30 days and never used for training.

Questions

Before you connect.

Which Slack permissions do you request?—

Three scopes: read channel names, post messages, and upload files. We never read message history, and you can revoke access from your Slack app settings.

How long does a render take?—

40–90 seconds for a 5–12 second clip at 1080p. A 4K upscale adds roughly two minutes. Queue position is shown while you wait, and the alert arrives whether the tab is open or not.

Can it post to a private channel?—

Yes, once the VidSlack app is invited to that channel. Private destinations are available on Scale.

What happens to my prompts and uploads?—

Stored for 30 days so you can re-download or re-run, then deleted. Nothing you upload or write is used to train models.

Do I need a workspace admin to install it?—

Only if your workspace requires app approval. In that case the connect flow generates a request your admin can approve in one click.

Which formats come back?—

MP4 (H.264) at 9:16, 1:1 or 16:9, up to 12 seconds, plus a JPG cover frame attached to the same message.
